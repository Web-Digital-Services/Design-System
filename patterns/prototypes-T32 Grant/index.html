About Training Grants

An institutional research training grant is an award that provides funding to support research training and education programs for trainees at the undergraduate, graduate, and postdoctoral levels. Training grants are an important source of support for research training programs, and help to ensure adequate numbers of highly trained scientists to assume leadership roles related to the Nation’s biomedical and behavioral research agenda. For National Institutes of Health (NIH) funded training grants, the focus of training activities can be in basic biomedical or clinical sciences, in behavioral or social sciences, in health services research, or in any other discipline relevant to the mission of NIH. Training grants provide support for stipends, tuition and fees, training-related expenses, and health insurance. Training grants are awarded to institutions, rather than individual researchers, and the institutions are responsible for overseeing the administration of the training program and selecting trainees who will participate. Training programs can include a variety of activities, such as research rotations, coursework, and mentored research projects. Training grants are usually awarded for a period of five years, and are renewable, subject to the availability of funding and demonstration of a strong program.

Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences (GSBS) NIH T32 Training Grant Support

The Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences (GSBS) is dedicated to supporting faculty looking to apply for an Institutional National Research Service Award (T32). These grants play a vital role in developing the next generation of researchers at UT Health San Antonio by providing structured, high-quality research training opportunities for graduate students and postdoctoral fellows.

Institutional training grants are designed to strengthen research training programs and ensure a pipeline of well-prepared scientists across biomedical, clinical, behavioral, and health services disciplines. These awards support stipends, tuition, health insurance, and training-related expenses, while fostering mentored research, coursework, and collaborative learning experiences. As institutional awards, T32 programs are administered at the program level, with faculty leading trainee selection and program oversight.

How GSBS Supports You

GSBS offers the following targeted support to help streamline and strengthen your T32 application:

  • Development of the required 10-page Institutional Support Letter
  • Preparation of key data tables: Table 1, Table 2, Table 3, Table 4, and Table 6A

We are actively expanding our services and are committed to partnering with you to enhance the competitiveness and success of your training program.

To allow sufficient time for comprehensive support, requests should be submitted at least six weeks prior to the application deadline.
