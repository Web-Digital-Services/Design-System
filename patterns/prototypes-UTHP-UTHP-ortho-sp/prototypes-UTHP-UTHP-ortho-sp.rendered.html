Nationally Recognized Leaders in Orthopaedics

We are San Antonio’s academic health center for specialized orthopaedic care. Our nationally recognized experts treat everything from joint and spine conditions to orthopaedic emergencies, with access to leading-edge procedures, clinical trials and coordinated rehabilitation.

We also care for athletes at every level across South Texas, including as team doctors for the San Antonio Spurs, UTSA Athletics and San Antonio Missions.

Why choose UT Health Orthopaedics

Our community relies on us for many reasons, including our:

  • Innovation that expands treatment options: We are the only academic health center in San Antonio. Our doctors advance orthopaedic care through medical research, clinical trials and new surgical and nonsurgical treatment. They train the next generation of doctors to continue this legacy of exceptional care.
  • Leading expertise: Our doctors set the standard for orthopaedic care through their extensive research and clinical experience. They bring nationally recognized expertise to every type of orthopaedic procedure, from common injuries to complex conditions. With our team, you find coordinated, patient-centered care supported by innovative research, advanced technology and decades of collective clinical experience.
  • Care trusted by elite athletes: We work with athletes at all levels of sport, and we are also the team physicians for the San Antonio Spurs, UTSA Athletics and the San Antonio Missions. Our doctors treat the best of the best and bring the same level of exceptional care to you.

UT Health San Antonio Sports Partnerships

UT Health San Antonio has partnered with the San Antonio Spurs in a joint effort to pursue innovations in human performance and improve the health of the greater San Antonio community.

We are making lives better by offering advanced sports medicine and orthopaedic care for our athletes and our community at locations across San Antonio and the Hill Country. 

UT Health San Antonio is proud to be the official sports medicine partner of the San Antonio Missions Baseball Club. Our expert providers deliver comprehensive care—helping players stay healthy and perform at their best.
