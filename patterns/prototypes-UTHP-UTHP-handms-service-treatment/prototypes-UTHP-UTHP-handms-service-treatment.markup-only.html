Hand and Microsurgery

Orthopaedics

Related to: Hand plastic surgery, orthopaedics

UT Health San Antonio provides specialized care for patients with hand and upper-extremity injuries and conditions. Our team of specialists provides comprehensive, individualized care designed to achieve the best results.

Whether you’ve been injured on the job, while playing sports or have ongoing pain and discomfort, we are here to help you regain proper use of your hands, arms, and elbows. Our physicians will assess your condition and provide you with the most advanced treatment options.
