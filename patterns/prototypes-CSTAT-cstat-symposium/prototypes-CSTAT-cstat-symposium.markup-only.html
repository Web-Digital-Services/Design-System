Texas Substance Use Symposium

Mark your calendars! TxSUS 2026 will be held March 2nd and 3rd in San Antonio, TX!

The mission of the Texas Substance Use Symposium, or TxSUS (pronounced “Texas”) is to reduce the burden of substance use on Texas communities by convening over 900 health care providers, public safety, behavioral health professionals, and peer recovery specialists to collaboratively identify our local substance use challenges and solutions to this public health concern.

TxSUS offers a continuing education curriculum that includes specialized learning opportunities and skill-building workshops led by high-quality instructors. In addition to a multitude of learning sessions, attendees will have opportunities to network and build partnerships with national and Texas-based substance use experts.
