IT Partner Directory
  • Research Systems

    The Research Systems page at UT Health San Antonio is a central hub linking researchers to the suite of administrative and scientific tools that support proposal development, study management, data capture, reporting, and institutional workflows. It streamlines access to key systems like ERMS, REDCap, iLab, Velos, and others that facilitate efficient research administration and execution across the institution.

    Learn more about Research Systems

  • Business Systems Services

    Business Systems Services provides maintenance of day-to-day operations of enterprise administrative systems which serve faculty, students, and staff. The services and expertise include Database Administration, Enterprise System Maintenance and Production Support, Application Access Management, Application Security Management, Accounts Management, Reporting and Analytics, and Integration Services.

    Visit the BSS page

  • Business Applications

    Access the tools and applications that keep our university running. This page connects faculty and staff to essential systems for managing business operations, communication, and daily workflows.

    View all applications

  • Cloud Research Infrastructure & Data Management (CRID)

    The Cloud Research Infrastructure & Data Management (CRID) page at UT Health San Antonio highlights the centralized services and support the institution provides for research computing, cloud resources, storage, and data management. It connects researchers with tools like Azure and AWS cloud support, high-performance computing, secure storage options, and expert technical assistance to power data-intensive projects.

    View all CRID tools

  • Access Control Executive (PeopleSoft)

    The ACE oversees departmental access to key administrative business systems such as PeopleSoft, the Document Review System (DRS), Data Warehouse, and eShipGlobal—all of which contain sensitive data vital to UT Health San Antonio operations.

    View all ACE tools
