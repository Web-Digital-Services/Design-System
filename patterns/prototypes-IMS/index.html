Our Mission

UT Health San Antonio Information Technology (UTHSA-IT) provides leadership in technology and information based resources and services for UT Health San Antonio. UTHSA-IT incorporates customer input to establish policies and standards while providing oversight, advocacy and solutions to support the institution in pursuit of its missions. UTHSA-IT will also serve as the primary institutional representative to The University of Texas System and state agencies with regard to information technology and information resources.

  • Business Systems Services

    Business Systems Services provides maintenance of day-to-day operations of enterprise administrative systems which serve faculty, students, and staff. The services and expertise include Database Administration, Enterprise System Maintenance and Production Support, Application Access Management, Application Security Management, Accounts Management, Reporting and Analytics, and Integration Services. Sherlie Schickedanz leads Business Systems Services and can be reached at 562-6419 or by email at Schickedanz@uthscsa.edu​.

  • Enterprise System Operations (Operations)

    Enterprise System Operations (Operations) is exclusively responsible for all central data center facilities and provides administration for all computing and storage devices that host or provide access to centralized resources, data or applications. Operations is the Custodian for all server and storage Information Resources unless explicitly assigned to an alternate Department or User by the Information Resource Owner. Operations shall actively monitor the performance and availability of all Information Resources installed in data center facilities, act as an initial point of escalation for support of server and storage resources, proxy interconnectivity between internal and external server and define administration standards for decentralized IT. Carol Nava leads Operations and can be reached at 567-2097 or by email at Nava@uthscsa.edu.

    Operations website

  • UTHSA-IT Business Office

    The UTHSA-IT Business Office (BO) manages the financial operations and provides oversight for the financial and administrative processes for UTHSA-IT. The BO is charged with the responsibility affecting five areas: accounting, billing, financial management, budget and research, and process improvement. The BO is also charged with the responsibility of Access Control Executive for UTHSA-IT and acts as the Executive Committee representative in matters of financial and administrative nature. Sherry Rime leads the Business Office and can be reached at 567-2191 or by email at Rime@uthscsa.edu.

  • Information Technology Service Management (ITSM)

    Information Technology Service Management (ITSM) provides hardware and software incident support and performs service configuration requests for Information Resources and computing devices owned, leased or under the control of the Health Science Center. ITSM is the Custodian for all Computing Devices unless explicitly assigned to an alternate Department or User by the Information Resource Owner. ITSM includes a centralized Service Desk, provides hardware support and sales for PC, Apple/MAC desktops, laptops, tablets, and peripherals. John Garcia leads ITSM and can be reached at 450-5395 or by email at Garciaj10@uthscsa.edu.

  • Infrastructure and Security Engineering (ISE)

    Infrastructure and Security Engineering (ISE) is exclusively responsible for the Health Science Center’s Network Infrastructure including, but not limited to, the local area network, wide area and telecommunications network, Internet, OTS network and wireless/wifi networks. ISE is explicitly responsible for engineering and administration of all security technology platforms, security monitoring operations, security incident management and information security operations as assigned by the Chief Information Security Officer. Jay Villarreal leads ISE and can be reached at 450-4150 or by email at VillarrealJM@uthscsa.edu.

  • Project Portfolio Management and Delivery

    This service offers project leadership and support to Health Science Center organizations desiring to launch and execute initiatives to improve ​processes and/or implement new processes involving technology. The project delivery service provides the requesting organization support in the following areas: Process and Systems Analysis, Project Management, Software Configuration and Development, Enterprise Business Systems Training and Development, User Acceptance Testing Management and Facilitation, Organizational Change Management and Communications to transition from implementation to sustainment. Bryan Menn leads the Project Portfolio Managment and Delivery teams and can be reached at 562-6433 or by email at Mennb@uthscsa.edu.

  • ​S​ystems Planning and Engineering (SPE)

    Systems Planning and Engineering (SPE) is responsible for the architecture, implementation and integration of enterprise compute and storage platforms. SPE will lead Proof-of-Concept testing and analysis of emerging technology platforms and services. Chuntida Harinnitisuk leads SPE and can be reached at 562-5604 or by email at Harinnistisuk@uthscsa.edu​.

  • Web Initiatives

    The Web Initiatives Department provides services for planning, design, development, assessment and ongoing enhancement of online properties for the institution, departments, business units or projects. We help organizations to assess their own digital goals, the audiences they need to connect with online to achieve those goals, and the behavior and needs of those audiences. Based on those insights, we help plan digital architecture, design online prototypes and interfaces, and build them into websites and/or online applications. We then continue to support online products through testing, launch, evaluation, maintenance and ongoing enhancement. For more information or to request help, contact webteam@uthscsa.edu​.

Students

IMS provides technology tools and support to help students succeed in their academic journey. From campus Wi-Fi and printing to software access and training, we make it easier to learn, connect, and collaborate on campus or online.

Employees

Clinical

  • Clinical support

Guests

Project Process & Guidance

Portfolio Management (PREP)

PREP stands for Project & Resource Engagement Process​​ and service as a centralized project request and prioritization process supported by a governance framework to align people and financial resources to projects supporting our strategic objectives.​​

Learn more about PREP

Project Initiation

All projects that cross departments for resources or assistance should use the formal process of project governance and oversight to maintain continuity across processes and more effectively manage the project from a risk and business complexity associated with projects. The Project and Resource Engagement Process (PREP) is the name of the project request process and is managed within TeamDynamix.

A Project Request should be initiated by submitting a request via the Create New Project Request service in TeamDynamix.​​

Learn more about initiation

Project Planning

During project planning, the project scope is defined and described with greater specificity as more information about the project is known. Existing risks, assumptions, and constraints are analyzed for completeness and added or updated as necessary.​

Learn more about planning

Project Execution

Project Management Office (PMO)

The PMO uses a SharePoint list called Issue Tracker to track project issues.

Issue tracking is necessary to identify and track to resolve project issues. Issues can be as simple as questions and as complex as a testing issue requiring coding and/or process fix.​

Learn more about project execution

Project Deployment

Deployment typically occurs after User Acceptance Testing is complete. During this stage, cutover activities such as stakeholder training/communication, CAB submission/approval, environment preparation for go-live, and go-live occur. On some projects, multiple deployment phases/waves may occur to minimize disruption to stakeholders and reduce the impact to project support staff and IMS Service Desk.​

Learn more about project deployment

Project Closeout

Project Closeout marks the completion of all project phases and ensures a smooth transition to ongoing operations. Activities include finalizing financials, verifying outcomes, transferring knowledge to sustainment teams, and documenting lessons learned. The process also confirms schedule completion, communicates results to stakeholders, and concludes with formal approvals and team recognition for a successful delivery.​

Learn more about project closeout
