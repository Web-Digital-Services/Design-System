Technology & Support

IMS connects the UT Health San Antonio community with essential IT services and resources. From troubleshooting and device setup to software access and training, IMS provides the tools and guidance to keep your work running smoothly.

Students

IMS provides technology tools and support to help students succeed in their academic journey. From campus Wi-Fi and printing to software access and training, we make it easier to learn, connect, and collaborate on campus or online.

  • Student support

Employees

IMS delivers reliable technology services that keep university operations running smoothly. We support workstations, business systems, communications tools, and secure network access so employees can focus on their roles across the institution.

  • Employee support

Faculty

IMS supports faculty with teaching, research, and collaboration technologies. Our services include classroom technology, research computing, and online learning tools designed to enhance instruction and advance discovery.

  • Faculty support

Clinical

IMS partners with clinical teams to ensure technology supports safe, efficient patient care. From EPIC training and device integration to secure data systems, we help streamline workflows and maintain compliance in clinical environments.

  • Clinical support

Guests

IMS provides limited network and technology access for visitors collaborating with UT Health San Antonio. Guest Wi-Fi and temporary accounts offer a secure way to stay connected during campus events, meetings, or research visits.

  • Guest support
