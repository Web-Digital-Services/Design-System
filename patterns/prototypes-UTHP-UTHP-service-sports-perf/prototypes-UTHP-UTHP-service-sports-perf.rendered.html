Sports Performance Training

Orthopaedics

What is sports performance training?

Sports performance training is a specialized sports medicine program designed to help youth and adult athletes achieve their desired performance potential. The program’s focus on proper conditioning and movements helps improve speed, strength and agility while lowering the risk of injury.

Why choose UT Health San Antonio for sports performance training?

At UT Health San Antonio, our athletic preparation physical therapists and certified athletic trainers partner with sports medicine specialists to provide customized performance plans for athletes of all ages, levels and sports. We tailor your program to help you achieve your goals — whether you compete on an elite level, enjoy recreational sports, plan to run a marathon or simply want to get into better shape.

As the official sports medicine partner of the San Antonio Spurs and the San Antonio Missions ballclub, and the official healthcare partner of The University of Texas at San Antonio (UTSA) Athletics, we have the expertise to help you succeed.

What are the benefits of sports performance training?

Benefits of participating in our sports performance training include:

  • Advanced athletic skills: Our program focuses on building speed, agility, strength and endurance. Improvements in these areas can help you perform better in your sport.
  • Injury prevention: Our training program helps strengthen muscles and improve flexibility, which can lower your risk of overuse injuries and sports-related injuries.
  • Performance rehabilitation: We offer performance rehabilitation for athletes recovering from sports injuries. Our team closely monitors your recovery and training, ensuring that you can safely return to activities in a way that lowers your risk of reinjury.

What to expect with sports performance training

At your initial appointment, your team will learn more about your current fitness level, athletic goals and medical history, including previous injuries or physical limitations. You may perform certain exercises to help your team assess your:

  • Balance and coordination
  • Joint flexibility and stability
  • Muscle strength
  • Walking and running gait

Your personalized training plan

Based on the assessment, your team will work with you to develop a personalized training plan. During each training session, you get hands-on guidance and support to ensure that you perform the exercises safely and correctly. Your team will monitor your progress and adjust the training plan to keep you on track to achieve your goals.

Your sports performance training plan may focus on:

  • Balance and coordination: Exercises that improve balance, coordination, agility and reaction time can lead to better performance in your sport.
  • Functional movements: Functional movement exercises mimic actions specific to your sport. They help improve your techniques so you can move with more power, precision and efficiency.
  • Mobility: Stretching, warmup and cooldown exercises help improve joint flexibility and range of motion. These exercises are important for preventing injuries.
  • Plyometrics: Fast, explosive exercises help build endurance. They also help you learn how to safely run, pivot, jump and land while minimizing injury risk.
  • Strength training: Our gradual power-loading approach improves core strength, muscle strength, power and endurance.

Education and support for athletes

We take a whole-athlete approach that focuses on all aspects of your physical and mental health. You have access to:

  • Mental health support with our sports psychologists and other providers
  • Nutrition counseling to make sure you properly fuel and hydrate while training and competing
  • Sports injury prevention services to promote safe physical activities

Your sports performance training care team

Our performance training team works closely with coaches and athletic trainers at schools and in club sports. You may also receive care from UT Health San Antonio specialists including:

  • Orthopaedic doctors and surgeons
  • Physical medicine and rehabilitation specialists (physiatrists)
  • Primary care providers
  • Sports medicine providers

Related Conditions

  • Overuse injury
  • Sports-related injury
