Medications for Opioid Use Disorder (MOUD) Mentorship Program

The Center for Substance Use Training & Telementoring, in collaboration with University Health, offers no-cost mentoring to prescribers located in the Texas Public Health District Region 8 looking to improve care for patients with opioid use disorder (OUD).

One-on-One Mentoring

Our program supports the use of effective, evidence-based medications, helps address implementation challenges, and provides complementary case consultations to strengthen clinical confidence and patient outcomes.

Who is this for?

  • Licensed Prescribers (e.g., MD, NP, PA, DO, etc.) who are currently prescribing medications for opioid use disorder (MOUD) or are interested in beginning to do so.
  • Providers located in the Texas Public Health District Region 8
