About the Office of Innovation & Strategic Partnerships

The Office of Innovation & Strategic Partnerships (OISP) supports UT San Antonio faculty and researchers in advancing discoveries toward real-world impact. Through education, strategic guidance, and collaborative partnerships, OISP helps translate innovation into solutions that improve lives.

Our Mission

The Office of Innovation & Strategic Partnerships supports UT San Antonio faculty and researchers in advancing innovations from discovery toward impact.

By emphasizing early market insight, experiential education, and collaborative partnerships, OISP helps innovators make informed decisions about commercialization pathways that create value beyond the university.

Our Core Values

  • Discover — Identifying promising innovations and unmet needs
  • Protect — Safeguarding intellectual property and research assets
  • Develop — Advancing innovations through education and guidance
  • Execute — Supporting pathways to commercialization
  • Impact — Creating real-world outcomes that improve lives

These values guide our work and decision-making across all programs.

Our Approach

OISP applies a Market-First approach to commercialization — emphasizing early market insight, customer discovery, and value validation to guide innovation development.

This approach helps faculty align research strengths with unmet needs, prioritize opportunities with the greatest potential, and pursue commercialization pathways grounded in evidence rather than assumption.

Education & Development Opportunities

OISP offers education and development programs designed to help faculty and research teams explore the commercial potential of their innovations. From early-stage market discovery to mentorship and venture development, these programs support innovators at every stage of the journey.

Programs are available to faculty, researchers, and select graduate students.

  • Founders Elect
  • Commercialization Workshop Series
  • Entrepreneur In-Residence Program
  • IdeaLaunch – Southwest I-Corps
  • Internship Opportunities

Founders Elect

UT San Antonio’s flagship faculty startup formation program, offering standardized terms and two flexible pathways to take your innovation from invention disclosure to operating company.

Learn More

Commercialization Workshops

Hands-on workshops that introduce faculty to market-first thinking, customer discovery, commercialization pathways, including licensing and startup options.

Learn More

Entrepreneur-in-Resident (EIR) Program

One-on-one guidance from experienced entrepreneurs to help faculty assess commercial strategy, refine value propositions, and navigate next steps toward impact.

Learn More

NSF I-Corps Southwest IdeaLaunch

A nationally recognized program that provides structured customer discovery training and funding, helping faculty teams validate real-world needs and de-risk their path to commercialization.

Learn More

Business Development Internship Program

Hands-on experiential learning where graduate-level interns work with OISP’s Market Opportunity & Development team to evaluate technologies, conduct market analyses, and support early-stage business planning.

Learn More

How These Programs Fit Together

These programs are designed to work together — not in isolation. Faculty and research teams may enter at different points depending on the maturity of their innovation and progress through multiple programs as their commercialization efforts evolve.

  • Early exploration: Commercialization Workshops
  • Market validation: NSF I-Corps
  • Strategic guidance: Entrepreneur-in-Resident (EIR) Program
  • Company Formation: Founders Elect

Industry & Partnership Engagement

The Office of Innovation & Strategic Partnerships works with industry leaders, entrepreneurs, investors, and subject matter experts to advance UT San Antonio innovations toward real-world impact. Through a variety of engagement opportunities, OISP connects external expertise with faculty innovation to strengthen development, inform strategy, and accelerate commercialization.

Ways to Engage

OISP offers multiple pathways for industry and entrepreneurial leaders to engage with the university — whether through mentorship, advisory roles, program participation, or strategic collaboration.

Workshop Subject Matter Experts

OISP regularly engages industry professionals as guest speakers and subject matter experts within its commercialization and startup education programs.

These engagements provide faculty and research teams with real-world perspectives while offering experts an opportunity to contribute to innovation education and ecosystem development.

Express Interest

Strategic Alliances & Partnerships

OISP builds and manages strategic relationships with external partners to advance institutional priorities, expand innovation pathways, and support commercialization and programmatic goals.

Through collaborative partnerships, OISP works to align university innovation with industry needs, shared objectives, and long-term impact opportunities.

Express Interest

Key Opinion Leader (KOL) Network

Aligned with OISP’s goal of advancing innovations toward patient and societal benefit, the KOL Network is comprised of industry, clinical, and venture professionals who bring real-world perspective to emerging technologies.

KOLs share subject matter expertise, market insight, and strategic feedback to help assess opportunity potential, inform development direction, and strengthen pathways to commercialization.

Become a Key Opinion Leader

Entrepreneur-in-Resident (EIR) Program

OISP’s Entrepreneur-in-Resident Program connects experienced entrepreneurs with faculty innovations to support the translation of research into market-ready solutions.

Entrepreneurs-in-Resident collaborate with faculty teams to assess commercial strategy, refine value propositions, and guide early venture development — while faculty remain in their academic roles.

This program offers entrepreneurs a unique opportunity to engage with cutting-edge research, contribute expertise, and help shape the next generation of innovation.

Through collaborative partnerships, OISP works to align university innovation with industry needs, shared objectives, and long-term impact opportunities.

About the Program

A Faculty-First Pathway to Launch

Founders Elect is UT San Antonio’s flagship faculty startup formation program — a structured, transparent pathway from invention disclosure to operating company. Modeled on Imperial College London’s internationally recognized Founders Choice framework, the program is designed for the unified research and health sciences university that UTSA and UT Health San Antonio have become.

The program gives faculty inventors clear, standardized, and well-supported options so that the journey from discovery to company is predictable, equitable, and efficient — regardless of where a founder is on their entrepreneurial journey.

Program Structure

Two Pathways. One Program.

Founders Elect offers two clearly defined routes. Choose based on how much control you want to retain and how much direct support you need from OISP’s commercialization team.

1. Founder Path

High Equity · Foundational Support

Designed for researchers who are ready to take the wheel. You retain more control and equity — in exchange for taking greater responsibility for your spinout’s development.

  • Standard option & license terms
  • Company formation support
  • IP maintenance on foundational tech
  • Investor showcase events
  • Incubator space introductions
  • Market assessment reports

2. Partnership Path

Shared Equity · Enhanced Support

Designed for researchers who want experienced hands alongside them. OISP’s commercialization team works the market and provides the support to reach investor-ready inflection points.

  • Everything in Founder Path, plus:
  • OISP-led deal management
  • Access to EIRs and Mentors
  • Warm investor introductions
  • Pitch and negotiation support
  • Strategic Alliance MOU option

Standard terms by design. Founders Elect uses standardized option and technology license terms — eliminating the need for founders to appoint an independent negotiator and enabling a streamlined timeline from disclosure to executed agreement.

University Equity

Transparent Equity Terms

The University will receive founding equity in spinout companies in exchange for its foundational IP, innovation ecosystem, research facilities, and project funding. Terms are fixed and published — no negotiation required.

Financial Terms

Licensing & Fee Structure

  • Licensing Fee

    Founder Path

    Execution Fee: $5,000 (due within 30 days of license effective date)

    Milestone Fee: $20,000 (due upon cumulative raise of $500,000)

    $500 credit available for completing an eligible OISP workshop.

    Partnership Path

    $50,000 one-time payment due on the second anniversary of the license effective date.

  • Royalty Rates

    Both paths use the same royalty rate schedule, applied on net sales of licensed patents. No royalty is due in the first year of sales (up to $1M cumulative net sales).

    Rate Schedule

    • 0.5% — Low margin businesses
    • 1.0% — Medium margin businesses
    • 2.0% — High margin businesses

    Margin Examples

    • Low: Commodity chemicals, mass manufacture (<10% net profit)
    • High: Medtech, biotech, pharma, SaaS (>15% net profit)
  • Sublicensing Fees

    Founder Path

    • 10% (pharmaceuticals)
    • 5% (non-pharmaceuticals)

    Of any non-royalty consideration received.

    Partnership Path

    • 15% (pharmaceuticals, may be tiered)
    • 10% (non-pharmaceuticals)

    Of any non-royalty consideration received.

  • Legal Expense Reimbursement

    Both Paths – Same Terms

    • Ongoing expenses: payable within 30 days of invoice
    • Past expenses: 5 equal annual installments; first payment on 1st anniversary of license
    • Patent fee deferral available during first year of Option Period (domestic filings only)

    Fee Deferral Note

    Foreign (non-U.S.) patent expenses are not eligible for deferral. PCT national/regional stage fees must be pre-paid no later than 60 days before the filing deadline.

How It Works

From Disclosure to Company

The Founders Elect process is designed to be fast and friction-free, with legal template documents available at each stage.

  1. Submit an Invention Disclosure

    File a disclosure with OISP. Your assigned program manager will conduct an initial review and reach out within 10 business days.

  2. Market & Commercialization Assessment

    OISP conducts an up-front assessment of market opportunity and competitive landscape. You receive a research report to inform your path selection.

  3. Select Your Path

    Choose Founder Path (high equity, foundational support) or Partnership Path (shared equity, enhanced OISP engagement). Path-specific terms apply from this point.

  4. Execute Option Agreement

    Sign the standard two-year option agreement (with the License Agreement attached as an exhibit). Company formation support begins.

  5. Incorporate & Operate

    OISP supports company incorporation using template documents, IP maintenance on your foundational technology, and introductions to incubator space and the investor community.

  6. Execute License & Launch

    Execute the definitive license agreement. University equity is issued. You are now an operating startup with OISP as an ongoing institutional partner.
