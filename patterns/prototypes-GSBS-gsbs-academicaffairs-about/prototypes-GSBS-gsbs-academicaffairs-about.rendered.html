Message from Senior Associate Dean, Dr. Timothy Raabe:

The Office of Academic Affairs in the Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences (GSBS) at UT San Antonio is here to support students throughout their academic journey. We work closely with students, faculty, and program leadership to foster a strong, supportive learning environment—from the moment students matriculate through the completion of their degrees. Our goal is to help ensure high-quality educational experiences that promote success, growth, and continuous improvement across the GSBS community.

This website serves as a central hub for the resources and tools you need, including academic policies, course evaluations, data dashboards, GradTrac, syllabi, mentoring programs, training grant information, and assessment results. Whether you are a student navigating graduate training or a faculty member mentoring the next generation of scientists, Academic Affairs is committed to providing clear information, meaningful support, and transparent data to help you thrive.

UT San Antonio Health Science Center is the academic health center of The University of Texas at San Antonio. It includes schools of medicin, dentistry, nursing, health professions, graduate biomedical sciences and public health, and a clinical enterprise called UT Health San Antonio, which conducts 2.5 million patient visits yearly.
