Campus Computer Store

The Campus Computer Store offers students, faculty, and staff academic discounts on Apple, Dell, Microsoft, and a wide range of accessories and software. Our on-campus location makes it easy to shop for laptops, tablets, displays, and essential tech gear. We also provide access to popular software titles like Adobe, SPSS, and GraphPad for those not covered by university licenses.

Printing & Copy Services

UT Print offers printing, copying, and promotional item services for the UT Health San Antonio community. From offset printing and in-house bindery to shared copier solutions, we handle everything from business cards to full-color publications. We also provide branded apparel and promotional items, available in both in-stock and custom options.
