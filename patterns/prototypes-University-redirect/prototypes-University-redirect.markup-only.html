Important Update: Government Relations website moving to utsa.edu

As part of the ongoing integration between UT Health San Antonio and The University of Texas at San Antonio , the Government Relations website will be transitioning to utsa.edu/government-relations.

Website Transition Details

  • LNew Website Location: Government Relations content will be available on utsa.edu/government-relations
  • Effective Date: Dec 5th, 2026
  • Current Contact Information: [Phone Number / Email Address]
  • Website Visitors: Existing links and bookmarks will be redirected during the transition period.

This website migration is designed to provide a more unified and streamlined experience for our stakeholders, legislative partners, community members, and university constituents. Government Relations services, resources, and points of contact will remain available throughout the transition.

We encourage visitors to update any saved bookmarks once the new site is live. If you have questions or need assistance locating information during the migration, please contact us at https://www.utsa.edu/government-relations/contact.html. Thank you for your continued partnership and support as we move forward together as one institution.

UT San Antonio Health Science Center is the academic health center of The University of Texas at San Antonio. It includes schools of medicin, dentistry, nursing, health professions, graduate biomedical sciences and public health, and a clinical enterprise called UT Health San Antonio, which conducts 2.5 million patient visits yearly.
