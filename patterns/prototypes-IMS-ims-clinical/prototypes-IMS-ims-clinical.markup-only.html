  • Computers & Medical Devices
    IMS provides guidance and support for selecting, purchasing, and maintaining technology used across UT Health San Antonio clinic operations. From standard office computers to specialized medical devices, IMS ensures equipment meets institutional standards for security, compatibility, and performance. Our teams assist with setup, configuration, and lifecycle management to keep your systems reliable and compliant.

    Make request here

  • Project Portfolio Management & Delivery

    This service provides leadership and support for technology-driven process improvements across the Health Science Center. We help with project management, development, training, and testing while guiding change management for lasting success.

    Learn more about PPMD

  • Enterprise Systems & Operations

    Enterprise Systems & Operations (ESO) provides leadership in technology and information-based services that support the mission of UT Health San Antonio. ESO manages core infrastructure and enterprise systems, including cloud research data, server and storage solutions, and business and clinical application integrations. The team ensures secure, reliable technology that empowers research, education, and patient care across the university.

    ESO website

  • Clinical EPIC Education & Training

    IMS offers support for clinicians and staff who need access to EPIC training and resources. IMS coordinates training sessions, account access, and education materials to ensure users can confidently navigate the EPIC electronic health record system. Our goal is to help clinical teams use EPIC effectively to improve patient care, documentation, and workflow efficiency.

    Epic training request

  • Business Systems Services

    Business Systems Services provides maintenance of day-to-day operations of enterprise administrative systems which serve faculty, students, and staff. The services and expertise include Database Administration, Enterprise System Maintenance and Production Support, Application Access Management, Application Security Management, Accounts Management, Reporting and Analytics, and Integration Services.
