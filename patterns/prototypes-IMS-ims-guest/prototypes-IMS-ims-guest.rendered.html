  • Guest Wi-Fi

    Guest Wi-Fi provides visitors to UT Health San Antonio with temporary internet access while on campus. This network is intended for guests who do not have a UT Health account and need short-term connectivity for meetings, conferences, or collaborations. Access can be requested through a sponsoring department, ensuring a secure and reliable connection that protects institutional resources while keeping guests connected.
