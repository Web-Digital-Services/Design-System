The Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences (GSBS) provides coordinated support for faculty developing or renewing predoctoral NIH Ruth L. Kirschstein Institutional National Research Service Award (T32) applications. Our goal is to help investigators navigate training-grant requirements, develop strong and coherent training plans, and connect with institutional resources needed to prepare a competitive application.

Available services vary depending on the application, institutional data requirements, participating programs, and submission timeline. Investigators are strongly encouraged to engage GSBS early in the planning process.

Need GSBS Support?

Submit a single request and our team will connect you with the appropriate support for your T32 application.

Request T32 Support

Planning a T32 Application

T32 applications are training grants, not research-project grants. A competitive application must describe an integrated training environment that brings together program leadership, faculty mentors, trainee recruitment and selection, curriculum, research experiences, professional development, mentoring, program evaluation, trainee outcomes, and instruction in the responsible conduct of research.

Because many application components depend on institutional data and coordinated input from multiple units, applicants should begin planning well in advance of the NIH submission date. GSBS can help identify relevant resources, institutional information, and internal partners.

How GSBS Can Help

Select a topic below to learn more about the ways GSBS can support your T32 application.

  • NIH Training Data Tables

    GSBS can help applicants interpret the NIH training-table requirements, provide institutional data sources and graduate education data, and review tables for completeness and internal consistency. The program director/principal investigator remains responsible for validating all data and ensuring that the application follows the current NIH instructions.

  • SciENcv Support

    GSBS can direct applicants and participating faculty to NIH biosketch guidance and SciENcv resources, help coordinate requests for mentor information, and identify common completeness or formatting issues. Each faculty member remains responsible for reviewing and certifying the accuracy of their own information.

  • Training Plan and Institutional Information

    GSBS can provide or help coordinate institutional information related to the graduate training environment, trainee recruitment and selection, mentoring, career and professional development, trainee outcomes, program evaluation, and available student-support resources. Standard institutional language or supporting information may be available for selected sections.

  • Responsible Conduct of Research

    GSBS can provide information about relevant institutional education and resources in the responsible conduct of research. Applicants should align their plans with the current NIH policy and the requirements of the applicable Notice of Funding Opportunity.

  • Rigor and Reproducibility

    GSBS can provide information about relevant institutional education and resources for enhancing reproducibility through rigor and transparency. Applicants should align their plans with the current NIH policy and the requirements of the applicable Notice of Funding Opportunity.

  • Mentor Training

    GSBS can provide information about relevant institutional education and resources for mentor training. Applicants should align their plans with the current NIH policy and the requirements of the applicable Notice of Funding Opportunity.

  • Institutional Letters of Support

    When appropriate, GSBS can help identify the correct institutional contact, coordinate requests for standardized information, and support development of institutional letters. Availability and lead times depend on the nature of the request and the submission schedule.

When to Contact GSBS

Contact GSBS as early as possible, and at least six weeks before the application deadline, particularly when the application will require institutional trainee data, NIH training tables, descriptions of graduate education programs, letters of institutional support, or coordination across multiple departments or graduate programs.

To request assistance, please be prepared to provide:

  • Name of the program director/principal investigator and primary contact
  • Proposed training program title
  • New application or renewal
  • NIH institute or center and funding opportunity number
  • Anticipated NIH submission date
  • Participating departments, programs, and training faculty
  • Specific assistance, institutional data, or letters requested
  • Requested date for GSBS materials

NIH T32 Resources

Applicants should use the current NIH Notice of Funding Opportunity and NIH application instructions as the authoritative sources for requirements. The following links are provided for convenience:

  • Parent T32 Notice of Funding Opportunity (PA-25-168)
  • NIH Training Data Tables, Instructions, and Samples
  • SciENcv
  • NIH NRSA Stipend Levels and Fiscal Policy Summary
  • NIH Responsible Conduct of Research Resources
  • NIH Rigor and Reproducibility Resources

Not all NIH institutes and centers participate in the Parent T32 announcement, and participating institutes may have institute-specific requirements, research priorities, deadlines, or funding opportunities. Applicants should review the current announcement and consult the appropriate NIH program contact before preparing an application.

GSBS Institutional Resources

Additional UT San Antonio resources are available to authorized users and may include institutional templates, data-request instructions, standard descriptions, checklists, internal contacts, and examples.

Important Notice

NIH requirements, forms, policies, and funding levels may change. Applicants should consult the current Notice of Funding Opportunity and NIH application instructions for authoritative requirements. GSBS resources are intended to supplement, not replace, NIH guidance. Applicants are responsible for the accuracy, completeness, and timely submission of their applications.
